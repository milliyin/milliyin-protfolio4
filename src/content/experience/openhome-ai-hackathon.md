Great day at the OpenHome 1-Day AI Hackathon powered by Algoryc.

I built [**Orbit Voice**](https://github.com/openhome-dev/abilities/pull/328), an agentic voice assistant that tracks the International Space Station in real time. It could answer where the ISS is right now, who is on board, when it will pass over a city, how fast it is moving, and even fun questions like what the crew is eating by reasoning through live data sources before replying with a voice response.

We placed **Top 7**. Even without taking home the final prize, the experience was worth it for the chance to build agentic voice AI under pressure, collaborate closely with other student developers, and learn from a fast-moving hackathon environment.
